# V36.7 Recruiter Card Fields

A recruiter should be able to answer in seconds: who is this, what do they do, where are they, where did SourcingOS find them, what other public/contact signals exist, why did they surface, and what still needs verification.

Discovery cards therefore surface only observed data:
- source-provided avatar or initials fallback;
- display name;
- headline and organization;
- observed source location or `Location not observed`;
- source/profile link;
- source-specific public contact signals when present;
- observed skills/evidence;
- Review Ready / Promising — Verify / Held state and explanation.

No guessed LinkedIn, email, residence, clearance, tenure, or cross-source identity is permitted.
